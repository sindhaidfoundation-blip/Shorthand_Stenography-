PLD 2023 SC 542 • Supreme Court of Pakistan
Constitutional Petition • Fundamental Rights & Due Process (Art. 10-A)
verified Verified Law Report

"The petitioner's learned counsel strenuously contends that the impugned judgment of the learned High Court violates fundamental rights guaranteed under the Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan. It is a well-settled principle of law that statutory tribunals must exercise their jurisdiction within the four corners of law without arbitrary exercise of discretion. The doctrine of legitimate expectation and principles of natural justice, audi alteram partem, are an integral part of due process under Article 10-A. The revision petition is accordingly disposed of with direct instructions to the appellate forum for expeditious adjudication on merits."
